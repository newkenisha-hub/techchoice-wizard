
# 🧙‍♂️ TechChoice Wizard

**Find the perfect tech stack for your project with guided recommendations**

TechChoice Wizard is a beginner-friendly web application that helps developers make informed technology decisions through guided questionnaires and intelligent trade-off analysis. Instead of overwhelming users with generic comparisons, it provides personalized recommendations based on your specific needs, experience level, and project constraints.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎯 **Personalized Recommendations**
- **Context-Aware Scoring**: Recommendations change based on your experience level, project type, team size, and priorities
- **Weighted Decision Making**: Different factors are weighted based on your specific situation (e.g., job market matters more for career-focused developers)
- **No One-Size-Fits-All**: Same technology can be recommended or discouraged based on your unique context

### 🗣️ **Beginner-Friendly Approach**
- **Plain English Questions**: No technical jargon - questions are written for developers at any level
- **Guided Decision Process**: Step-by-step questionnaire eliminates decision paralysis
- **Educational Explanations**: Learn why certain technologies fit your needs better than others
- **Visual Progress Tracking**: Clear progress indicators show how far through the process you are

### 🔍 **Intelligent Trade-off Analysis**
- **"Why This?" Explanations**: Clear reasoning for top recommendations with specific benefits
- **"Why Not Others?" Insights**: Understand exactly why alternatives weren't recommended for your situation
- **Contextual Cons**: Disadvantages are explained relative to your specific needs, not generic weaknesses
- **Score Transparency**: See numerical scores (X/10) to understand recommendation confidence

### 🧠 **Smart Scoring Algorithm**

#### **Multi-Factor Analysis**
- **Experience Level**: Recommendations adapt to beginner, intermediate, or advanced skill levels
- **Project Constraints**: Timeline, budget, team size, and complexity all influence scoring
- **Priority Weighting**: Your stated priorities (learning, speed, performance, jobs) boost relevant scores
- **Category-Specific Logic**: Each tech category uses tailored scoring criteria

#### **Dynamic Weight Adjustment**
- **Project Type Influence**: Personal projects prioritize learning, startups prioritize speed, enterprise prioritizes scalability
- **Team Size Impact**: Solo developers get different recommendations than large teams
- **Experience Compensation**: Beginner-friendly options score higher for new developers

## 🚀 **Getting Started**

1. **Open `index.html`** in any modern web browser
2. **Choose a category** (Frontend, Backend, Database, or Cloud)
3. **Answer 4 simple questions** about your project and experience
4. **Get personalized recommendations** with clear explanations
5. **Understand the trade-offs** with "why not" reasoning for alternatives
